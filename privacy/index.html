Privacy notice

How we use your details

Last updated 25 November 2026. This notice follows the Nigeria Data Protection Act 2023 (NDPA), and the EU GDPR where it applies to you.

1. Who we are

Chada Tech (the Chada Tech Community, part of Chada Digital), Nigeria. Contact: [email to be added].

2. What we collect

  • Your name, WhatsApp number and email.
  • What you sell or do, and how you heard about us.
  • For the Student track: a proof of studentship (student ID or admission letter).
  • For the Reply Kit: your first name, WhatsApp number, business name, what you sell, city and chosen tone. The bank details you type into the Reply Kit never leave your phone. We never see or store them.

3. Why we use it (and our legal basis)

  • To register you for a class, process your payment and add you to classes and groups (to carry out what you asked for, and your consent).
  • To send free AI tips and class updates on WhatsApp, only if you ticked the box to agree. Reply STOP any time to stop them.

4. Who processes it for us

Supabase (our database), Paystack (payments) and our website host. They process data only on our instructions. Some of these providers may store data outside Nigeria, with safeguards required by law. We never sell your data.

5. How long we keep it

  • Free class and Reply Kit sign-ups: 12 months.
  • Member and payment records: as long as the law requires.
  • Student proofs: deleted 30 days after we check them.

6. Your rights

You can ask to see, correct or delete your data, object to how we use it, or withdraw your consent at any time. Message us on WhatsApp (our business number) or email us. You can also complain to the Nigeria Data Protection Commission (NDPC).

7. Security

Our database only lets visitors add a registration. Only signed-in Chada Tech team admins can read it. Student proofs are stored in a private folder.

8. Cookies and analytics

We use a privacy-friendly visit counter to see which pages and posts bring visitors. The Reply Kit remembers on your own device that you've unlocked it, so you don't fill the form again.
