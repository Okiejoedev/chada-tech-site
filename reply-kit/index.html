How to save these in WhatsApp Business

  1. Open WhatsApp Business → Settings → Business tools.
  2. Quick replies: tap +, paste a reply and use the shortcut shown (e.g. /price). In a chat, type / to use it.
  3. Greeting message and Away message: paste the messages from the Profile tab.
  4. Business profile: paste your business description.
  5. Catalog: add each product with a short description.
